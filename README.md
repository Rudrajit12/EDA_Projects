# 🚀 **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Mini Projects**

Welcome to the **EDA Mini Projects Repository**! This repository houses a collection of short yet impactful projects focused on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** across various domains such as automobiles, FMCG sales, and IMDB data. The goal is to extract meaningful insights from raw datasets, exploring patterns, trends, and correlations that lead to a deeper understanding of the data.

---

## 📁 **Projects Overview**

This repository includes the following EDA mini projects:

### 1. **🚗 Automobile Data EDA**
   - **Description**: This project dives into automobile data, analyzing variables such as prices, engine types, fuel efficiency, and horsepower. It aims to identify trends that influence car performance and market value.
   - **🔍 Key Insights**:
     - Pricing trends based on engine types and fuel efficiency.
     - Correlation between horsepower and vehicle pricing.
     - Popular car brands and their market performance.

---

### 2. **🏪 FMCG Sales Data EDA**
   - **Description**: This analysis focuses on sales data from the FMCG sector, uncovering patterns in product sales, regional performances, and seasonal trends. By understanding these factors, we can pinpoint drivers of high-performing product categories.
   - **🔍 Key Insights**:
     - Distribution of sales across product categories.
     - Regional performance and high-sales areas.
     - Seasonal sales patterns and trending products.

---

### 3. **🎬 IMDB Data EDA**
   - **Description**: An exploration of IMDB data, including movie ratings, genres, and box office performance. The analysis reveals trends in movie production, audience reception, and the factors behind successful films.
   - **🔍 Key Insights**:
     - Trends in movie ratings across different genres and time periods.
     - Correlation between movie budgets and box office revenue.
     - Top-rated actors, directors, and popular genres over time.
